Pricing Questions Answered

Solar system costs vary significantly based on your property size, roof type, location, chosen equipment, and energy goals. We provide accurate quotes after a free assessment so you get a figure that actually reflects your situation.

Key factors include system size, panel and inverter type, roof complexity, electrical upgrades needed, and local permitting costs. Our specialists will walk you through each factor during your free consultation.

Incentives vary by location and change frequently. We recommend consulting your local authority or a financial adviser for current incentive information — GreenGrid does not guarantee any specific rebate amounts. We're happy to point you in the right direction.

Financing options may be available depending on your location and eligibility. Contact us to discuss payment arrangements and we'll help explore the options that work best for you.

Our quotes typically include equipment supply, installation labor, standard mounting, electrical connection, and commissioning. Specific inclusions are detailed in your formal quotation so there are no surprises.
