Privacy Policy &
Data Protection

At GreenGrid Solar Energy Solutions, we believe technical integrity begins with respecting your personal information. Discover how your property, utility, and design data are safeguarded.

Last Revised: November 2026 CCPA / CPRA & GDPR Compliant 256-Bit Encrypted Architecture
Section 1.0

Information We Collect

To engineer high-efficiency photovoltaic systems and model reliable financial forecasts, GreenGrid collects technical, environmental, and contact details provided during site evaluations.

We collect information strictly necessary to design, permit, install, and service renewable energy equipment for residential homeowners and commercial enterprises.

  • Property & Structural Data: Roof pitch, azimuth orientation, roofing material, square footage, satellite imagery, and electrical panel service capacity.
  • Utility & Consumption Metrics: Historical 12-month kilowatt-hour (kWh) billing records and utility rate tariff schedules required for Net Metering calculations.
  • Contact & Identification Records: Full name, verified email address, telephone number, and physical installation address.
Zero Data Monetization Policy:

GreenGrid never sells, rents, or trades your contact details or electrical usage records to third-party telemarketers or advertisers.

Section 2.0

How Information Is Used & Shared

Solar energy installations require formal coordination with municipal Authorities Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) and regional utility grid operators.

Your information is shared exclusively with authorized regulatory bodies and certified logistics partners necessary to bring your system into compliant commercial operation:

  • Permit Processing: Submitting single-line diagrams, engineering plan-sets, and structural calculations to local city/county building and safety departments.
  • Interconnection Agreements: Registering your photovoltaic array with regional electricity providers for Permission to Operate (PTO) and export crediting.
  • Hardware Warranty Registration: Transmitting serial numbers to tier-1 module and inverter manufacturers to activate 25-year performance warranties.
Section 3.0

Your Privacy Rights (CCPA / GDPR)

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction, GreenGrid grants all visitors transparent control over their personal information and technical records.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you hold enforceable legal entitlements:

  • Right to Know & Access: You may request an exported copy of all technical assessments, proposals, and contact entries maintained on your profile.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request permanent deletion of marketing records, subject to legal requirements regarding completed building permits.
  • Cookie Opt-Out: We utilize essential technical cookies for dark/light themes and RTL preferences. No tracking pixels are deployed without consent.
Section 4.0

Security Standards & Retention

We deploy bank-grade cryptographic protections across all customer data pipelines, API integrations, and field audit uploads.

All stored files, CAD drawings, and customer correspondence are housed within ISO 27001-certified data centers utilizing AES-256 bit encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

Solar System Warranty Records:

Because GreenGrid guarantees 25-year photovoltaic module performance, critical system schematics, electrical line diagrams, and commissioning logs are archived for the full lifecycle of your installation to guarantee unbroken maintenance coverage.

Questions About Your Data Privacy?

Our Data Governance and Compliance Officer is available to review personal data requests, verify retention records, or assist with CCPA inquiries.
