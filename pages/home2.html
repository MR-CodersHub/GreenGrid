System Overview

What Makes Up a Solar System?

A complete solar energy system is made up of several key components working seamlessly together to generate, store, and deliver clean power to your home or business.

Solar Panels

High-efficiency monocrystalline or polycrystalline photovoltaic panels that convert sunlight directly into electricity for your property.

Inverters

Convert DC electricity generated by your solar panels into AC power that your appliances and the grid can use safely and efficiently.

Mounting Structures

Durable racking systems engineered to securely hold panels at the optimal angle, whether on rooftops, ground mounts, or carports.

Battery Storage

Lithium-ion battery banks that store surplus solar energy so you can power your property after dark or during grid outages.

Charge Controllers

Regulate the voltage and current flowing from your panels to the battery bank, protecting your storage system and maximising its lifespan.

Monitoring Systems

Smart apps and dashboards that give you real-time visibility into energy production, consumption, and system performance from anywhere.
