Proposals, Estimates & Modeling

Preliminary quotations generated through online calculators or preliminary consultations represent engineering estimates based on regional solar irradiance averages.

Actual solar electrical generation depends on uncontrollable environmental variables, including unseasonal cloud cover, micro-climate temperatures, physical obstructions, and utility tariff restructuring.

  • Site Feasibility Audits: Final binding pricing and guaranteed minimum system capacity require a physical or high-resolution LIDAR drone audit by a certified GreenGrid structural auditor.
  • Structural Upgrades: If pre-existing roof rafters, electrical subpanels, or utility service drops fail local building codes, required remediation costs will be itemized prior to contract execution.
  • Tax Credit Disclaimers: While GreenGrid systems qualify for the 30% Federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC), clients must consult licensed tax advisors regarding individual eligibility.
Clause 2.0

Permitting, AHJ & Grid Interconnection

GreenGrid acts as an authorized turnkey contractor managing municipal permits, engineering stamps, and electrical utility grid interconnection paperwork.

Operating a grid-tied solar photovoltaic installation requires regulatory approvals outside GreenGrid's unilateral control:

  • Municipal Jurisdiction Timelines: Processing schedules for building and electrical safety permits vary by local city jurisdictions, typically spanning 2 to 6 weeks.
  • Permission to Operate (PTO): The system must remain powered down until the regional utility completes its bi-directional meter upgrade and formally issues written PTO.
  • Anti-Islanding Safety: To protect utility lineworkers during grid outages, grid-tied inverters automatically disconnect within milliseconds unless paired with battery storage.
Clause 3.0

Warranties & Performance Guarantees

Every GreenGrid installation incorporates dual-layer protection: manufacturer hardware coverage coupled with our dedicated workmanship guarantee.

We partner exclusively with Bloomberg Tier-1 photovoltaic manufacturers upholding rigorous linear power production curves:

  • 25-Year Linear Power Warranty: Guaranteed minimum 98% nominal power in Year 1, with annual degradation strictly capped below 0.50% through Year 25.
  • 10-Year Roof Penetration Guarantee: GreenGrid warrants all roof mounting attachments against water intrusion and weather leakage for 10 full years post-activation.
  • Smart Inverter & Battery Storage: Inverters and lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) storage units carry 10-to-15 year factory warranties with real-time app telemetry.
Maintenance Exclusions:

Warranties exclude damage caused by unauthorized third-party modifications, extreme seismic occurrences, external projectile strikes, or failure to perform recommended bi-annual panel washing.

Clause 4.0

Payment Terms, Billing & Net Metering

Clear, transparent milestone-based invoicing protects both clients and technicians throughout the procurement and installation cycle.

GreenGrid offers diverse acquisition structures including direct cash purchase, low-interest loan financing, and $0-down solar lease arrangements:

  • Milestone Progress Invoicing: Standard contracts release payments across 3 milestones: Design Approval (10%), Mechanical Completion (70%), and Utility PTO (20%).
  • Net Billing Tariff Compliance: Energy export compensation rates are determined solely by regional public utility commissions under modern NEM 3.0 frameworks.
  • Late Assessments: Uncontested balances outstanding past 30 calendar days from formal PTO may incur statutory 1.5% monthly commercial interest.
