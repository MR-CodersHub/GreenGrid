Understanding Your Solar System

Choose from a range of high-performance solar technologies tailored to your home's energy profile and budget.

☀️

Monocrystalline Panels

Premium single-crystal silicon cells delivering the highest efficiency ratings available. Ideal for roofs with limited space that need maximum output.

🔆

Polycrystalline Panels

A cost-effective multi-crystal silicon option that provides reliable performance. A popular choice where roof space is not a constraint.

⚡

String Inverters

The industry-standard inverter type. Converts DC power from your panel array to AC electricity for home use. Straightforward and proven technology.

🔌

Microinverters

Installed on each panel individually for panel-level power optimisation. Reduces the impact of shading and allows granular performance monitoring.

How We Work

Our 6-Step Installation Process

From the first conversation to handover day — a seamless journey guided by certified GreenGrid professionals every step of the way.

  1. 1

    Consultation & Needs Assessment

    We begin with a no-obligation consultation to understand your energy goals, review your electricity bills, and discuss the right solar solution for your household. This helps us tailor a system designed around your actual needs.

  2. 2

    Site Inspection & Roof Analysis

    A certified GreenGrid technician visits your property to assess roof orientation, pitch, shading, and structural suitability. We use advanced tools to model your roof's solar potential throughout the year.

  3. 3

    Custom System Design

    Using site data, we engineer a bespoke system layout — panel placement, inverter selection, wiring routes, and estimated output. You receive a detailed proposal with component specifications before anything is ordered.

  4. 4

    Professional Installation

    Our accredited installation team handles everything — mounting structures, panel installation, inverter wiring, and grid-connection preparation. Most residential installations are completed within one to two days.

  5. 5

    Testing & Grid Connection

    Once installed, we conduct comprehensive electrical testing and system commissioning. We coordinate with your utility provider for the grid connection and metering setup required to begin exporting surplus energy.

  6. 6

    Handover & Ongoing Support

    We walk you through your monitoring app, explain system operation, and hand over all documentation. GreenGrid remains available for ongoing support, annual check-ups, and any performance questions you may have.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about residential solar installation with GreenGrid.

Most installations are completed within 1–3 days after all permits are secured. The permitting process itself can take additional weeks depending on your local authority — your GreenGrid project manager will keep you informed throughout.
Solar panels require minimal maintenance. Periodic cleaning — especially after dust storms or extended dry spells — and an annual professional inspection are typically recommended to maintain optimal performance. Your system's monitoring app will flag any performance anomalies.
Our team will analyse your electricity bills (12 months if possible), available roof space, roof orientation, and your energy goals to recommend an appropriate system size. We model projected output for your specific location before making a recommendation.
Standard grid-tied solar systems automatically switch off during grid outages for safety — this protects utility workers servicing the lines. Battery storage systems can provide backup power during outages. Ask us about our battery storage solutions to get the best of both worlds.
Warranties vary by manufacturer and product line. Contact GreenGrid for current warranty information on the specific panels, inverters, and mounting hardware included in your quote. We'll provide full warranty documentation before installation begins.
Our site assessment evaluates roof orientation, angle, shading from trees or nearby structures, and structural integrity. North-facing roofs (in the Southern Hemisphere) or south-facing roofs (in the Northern Hemisphere) with minimal shading are ideal, but many other configurations can still deliver excellent results.
