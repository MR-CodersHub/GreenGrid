Who We Are

Building a Cleaner Energy Future Since 2010

GreenGrid Solar Energy was founded in 2010 by a small group of engineers and clean-energy advocates who believed that renewable power should be accessible to every home and business — not just early adopters with deep pockets. Starting from a single workshop in California, we set out to bring honest, high-quality solar installations to our community at a fair price.

Over the past fifteen years, we have grown from that original team of five into a full-service solar company with more than 120 engineers, installers, and customer success specialists. We have completed over 500 residential and commercial installations across California and the Pacific Northwest, generating a combined capacity of more than 10 megawatts of clean, renewable power.

Our growth has never come at the cost of our values. We continue to maintain the same hands-on approach we practised on day one — listening carefully to each customer's energy goals, designing systems that precisely match their needs, and standing behind every installation with rigorous post-installation support. We do not believe in one-size-fits-all solar; we believe in solar that fits your life.

As utility rates rise and climate urgency grows, GreenGrid remains focused on what matters most: delivering proven, long-lasting solar solutions that make financial and environmental sense for the people and businesses we serve.

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To accelerate the transition to clean energy by making high-performance solar systems accessible, affordable, and understandable for every homeowner and business — delivered with the honesty, quality, and care that our customers deserve.

We exist to remove barriers to solar adoption: financial, technical, and informational. Every installation we complete is a step toward a grid powered entirely by renewable sources, and we take that responsibility seriously.

Our Vision

A world where clean, affordable solar energy powers every home and business — where communities are resilient, energy-independent, and free from the environmental and economic burdens of fossil fuels.

We envision GreenGrid as the trusted name that families and businesses turn to for life-long clean energy partnerships: from first consultation through decades of system performance, support, and upgrades.

Clean Energy Accessibility

We actively work to make solar adoption possible for a wider range of households and businesses, including flexible financing options and community programmes.

Sustainable Communities

Every system we install strengthens community energy resilience, reduces local carbon emissions, and contributes to a more sustainable region for future generations.

Responsible Practices

From the components we source to the waste we minimise on every job site, we hold ourselves to the highest standards of environmental and professional responsibility.

Our Standards

Our Commitment to Quality & Safety

Every GreenGrid installation is underpinned by industry-leading quality and safety standards — because your home, your business, and your family deserve nothing less.

Certified Installation Practices

All installations follow NEC, IEC, and local authority standards. Our installers hold current NABCEP and electrical trade certifications.

Premium Equipment Standards

We only partner with Tier-1 panel and inverter manufacturers whose products meet our rigorous performance, durability, and warranty criteria.

Rigorous Safety Protocols

Mandatory pre-work safety briefings, PPE requirements, roof load assessments, and electrical safety checks are carried out on every single job.

Quality Assurance Checks

Post-installation inspections, IV-curve testing, and thermal imaging confirm that every system performs exactly as designed before handover.

Ongoing Team Training

Our technicians complete regular skills updates, manufacturer training, and safety refreshers to stay current with evolving solar technologies.

Transparent Customer Communication

Detailed project updates, honest timelines, and plain-language explanations — we keep you fully informed at every stage of your installation.

Environment First

Our Commitment to a Greener Tomorrow

At GreenGrid, sustainability is not a marketing claim — it is the reason we exist. Every solar system we install directly reduces dependence on fossil-fuel-generated electricity, cutting greenhouse gas emissions and helping to stabilise our climate for future generations.

We actively pursue responsible practices throughout our own operations: minimising waste on job sites, recycling decommissioned panels, and sourcing components from manufacturers with strong environmental credentials. We believe that the companies building the clean energy transition must themselves lead by example.

Our community impact programmes support solar access for under-served households, school educational initiatives, and local clean energy advocacy — because a sustainable future must be an equitable one.
